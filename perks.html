Referral Program

Reward: Clients who refer a new client can receive up to $20 in service credits or gift cards. Referred new clients may also receive up to $10 off their training services or a $10 gift card.

Eligibility Requirement: The referred client must purchase a training package valued at $200 or more.

How to Claim: The new client must enter the referring client's name in the Referral section of their Client Intake Form at initial booking and the Trainer must be informed.

Program Terms: Applies to new clients only. Cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discounts exceeding 20% in total value. Issued at the Trainer's sole discretion via account credit, gift certificates, or a combination thereof. The Trainer reserves the right to modify or end promotional programs at any time without prior notice.

First Responder, Military & Educator Discount

Discount: Active-duty military, veterans, law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs, healthcare workers, and teachers receive 5% off standard training packages.

Eligibility Requirement: Client must purchase a full training package valued at $300 or more. Proof of ID, service, or employment is required prior to invoice payment.

How to Claim: Present valid ID or verification documentation to Trainer prior to invoice payment.

Program Terms: Applies to eligible personnel only. Cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discounts exceeding 20% in total value. The Trainer reserves the right to modify or end promotional programs at any time without prior notice.

Returning Client Discount

Discount: Returning clients receive 10% off any upgraded or higher-tier training package.

Eligibility Requirement: Client must have previously completed a full training package with Cooper Canines LLC.

How to Claim: Mention your prior package participation when requesting your next package intake or consultation.

Program Terms: Cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discounts exceeding 20% in total value. The Trainer reserves the right to modify or end promotional programs at any time without prior notice.
