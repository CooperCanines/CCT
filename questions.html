Frequently Asked Questions

What training methods do you use?

We utilize positive reinforcement methods tailored to your dog's unique personality and needs.

What should I bring to our first session?

Please bring high-value treats, a standard 6-foot leash, and your dog's favorite toy.

How long does each training program take?

Program lengths vary from 3-week basic obedience tracks to multi-month behavior modification plans.
