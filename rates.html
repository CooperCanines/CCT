Service Area & Travel Logistics

  • Core Service Area: Primary rates apply within Camden, Burlington, and Gloucester Counties.
  • Extended County Travel: An additional $25 travel surcharge applies to sessions conducted outside Camden, Burlington, or Gloucester Counties (in South Jersey).
  • Out-of-Region Travel: A $100 regional travel fee applies to locations outside South Jersey (separate from standard out-of-county travel rates).
  • Service Boundaries: In-person training is exclusively limited to South Jersey, Eastern Pennsylvania, and Delaware.

Package & Service Notes

* Rate Adjustments: All rate estimates are subject to travel distance, location requirements, and individual dog assessment. Final adjustments are confirmed prior to agreement.
* Ongoing Communication: Enrolled package clients are encouraged to reach out with brief training updates and questions between sessions.
