Additional Session Options

  • Follow-Up Sessions: $60 / hour (Exclusive to active clients who have completed a base package).
  • Individual Sessions: $150 / session (Standalone booking without package enrollment).
  • Complimentary Check-In: Package clients receive 1 free follow-up check-in session 3–4 weeks after package completion to review progress.

Service Areas Travel Surcharge

  • NJ: Burlington, Camden, & Gloucester Counties: Standard session rates apply.
  • NJ: Atlantic, Cumberland, & Salem Counties: Additional $25 per session.
  • NJ: Cape May & Ocean Counties: Additional $50 per session.
  • PA: Philadelphia County: Additional $25 per session.
  • PA: Bucks, Chester, Delaware, & Montgomery Counties: Additional $30 per session.
  • DE: New Castle County: Additional $30 per session.

Package & Service Notes

  • Consultation Credit: Consultation rates (excluding travel surcharge) are credited directly toward any package selection.
  • Customized Plans: Depending on initial assessment, behavioral complexity, or multi-dog homes, certain training goals may require additional or bundled packages to achieve optimal results.
  • Rate Adjustments: All rate estimates are subject to travel distance, location requirements, and individual dog assessment. Final adjustments are confirmed prior to agreement.
  • Ongoing Communication: Enrolled package clients are encouraged to reach out with brief training updates and questions between sessions.
Rates shown are estimated baselines and may vary depending on needs. Final pricing will be determined during consultation or planning.
