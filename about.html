About Cooper Canines

Force-Free, Science-Based Dog Training in Haddon Township & South Jersey

Training Philosophy

At Cooper Canines, the primary goal is to help you build mutual respect and clear communication with your dog without resorting to raised voices, intimidation, or painful corrections. Real, lasting behavior change happens when a dog actively wants to cooperate with you.

Training protocols are completely customized to fit your lifestyle, using flexible treat-based and non-treat positive reinforcement techniques. Whether you are establishing basic manners, addressing leash pulling, or building reliable focus, training is kept positive, clear, and effective.

Trainer's Commitment

To keep service high-quality and accessible while expanding program offerings, client introductory rates are kept transparent and competitive.

As part of an ongoing commitment to the South Jersey community, free public educational resources and behavioral guides will also be available soon at coopercanines.com/guides.

Meet Your Trainer

Hello! My name is Dan, and I am the owner and trainer behind Cooper Canines LLC. Growing up surrounded by dogs of all breeds and temperaments gave me an early appreciation for canine behavior and communication. That lifelong passion led me to pursue structured education in modern canine behavior, studying dog body language through comprehensive coursework, professional literature, and hands-on shadowing alongside experienced professional dog trainers.

In addition to running Cooper Canines, I have served as a first responder and firefighter since 2015. That background instills a high standard of trust, safety, patience, and clear communication—values that directly guide my approach to working with clients and their dogs.

  • Fully Insured Business: Dedicated to professional standards, liability protection, and peace of mind.
  • 100% Force-Free & Positive: Based on modern scientific research—no fear, intimidation, or physical punishment.
  • Private 1-on-1 Focus: Customized training sessions designed around your household’s specific goals.
  • Upfront, Transparent Pricing: Clear estimated rates with zero hidden fees or sales pressure.
