Puppy Potty Training: A Step-by-Step Guide

Published by Cooper Canines | Category: Puppy Care

Potty training requires consistency, patience, and positive reinforcement[cite: 1]. Establishing a predictable schedule early on helps your puppy understand what is expected of them.

1. Stick to a Schedule

Take your puppy outside at fixed times, especially after meals, naps, and play sessions.

2. Reward Immediately

Always praise and offer a high-value treat right after your puppy finishes eliminating outdoors.

How to Stop Your Dog From Pulling on the Leash

Published by Cooper Canines | Category: Loose Leash Walking

Leash pulling is a common challenge, but teaching your dog to walk calmly beside you is doable with positive reinforcement techniques[cite: 1].

1. Stop Moving When the Leash Pulls Taut

Become a statue whenever your dog pulls. Only move forward when the tension on the leash drops.

2. Use High-Value Treats

Reward your dog close to your hip to build value for staying near your side.

5 Essential Commands Every Dog Should Know

Published by Cooper Canines | Category: Obedience

Mastering fundamental commands builds communication and safety for your dog in daily life[cite: 1].

  • Sit: The foundation for basic manners.
  • Stay: Helps build impulse control.
  • Come (Recall): Vital for safety outdoors.
  • Down: Encourages calm behavior.
  • Leave It: Prevents ingestions of dangerous items.
